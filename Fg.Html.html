🔍 What is the ₦50,000 FG Grant?

The Presidential Conditional Grant Scheme (PCGS) is a Federal Government initiative under the Presidential Palliative Programme. It provides non-repayable grants of ₦50,000 to eligible nano businesses across Nigeria.

  • Target: Nano-businesses (under 4 employees, turnover below ₦1 million)
  • Amount: ₦50,000 per beneficiary, paid directly into bank accounts
  • Repayment: NOT a loan, it's a pure grant, no repayment required
  • Condition: Beneficiaries are expected to hire at least one additional worker

The scheme targets micro and small entrepreneurs, with a significant number of beneficiaries being women in informal and semi-formal sectors.

💰 Conditional Cash Transfers (CCT)

The flagship Conditional Cash Transfer programme, anchored on the National Social Register, has reached over 8.3 million households, supporting more than 35 million Nigerians.

  • Amount: ₦25,000 to ₦75,000 per vulnerable household
  • Duration: Typically disbursed over a three-month timeframe
  • Target: 15 million vulnerable households by 2027

The Federal Government allocated ₦202 billion to NSIPA programmes in the 2026 Appropriation Bill.

🎓 Student & Youth Grants

₦250,000 Unconditional Grants: President Tinubu has approved unconditional grants of ₦250,000 for outstanding MSMEs participating in Expanded National MSME Clinics across states. These are not loans, they are direct grants.

Student Venture Capital Grant (2026): From over 30,000 applications, only 45 beneficiaries were selected for the highly competitive S-VCG programme, receiving equity-free funding alongside mentorship, incubation support, and digital tools.

🏛️ How Much Did Tinubu Give Governors?

President Tinubu's administration has provided substantial support to state governments:

  • Monthly grants: ₦5 billion monthly to all 36 states and the FCT, initiated in 2023
  • Intervention funds: Reports indicate about ₦500 billion in intervention funds disbursed to states

Note: Some governors have alleged that only APC-controlled states are benefiting from certain funds, while the Presidency urges Nigerians to hold their governors accountable for proper utilization.
